    About this calendar

    How a fit is found

    For each payment, the calendar takes the latest saved maturity on or before that date. "Cash waits" counts calendar days from that maturity to the payment; no return is assumed for those days. The first maturity after the payment is shown for comparison: holding it would mean selling before maturity at an unknown price.

    Fixed and price-sensitive payments

    A fixed USD payment needs the dollars on time, so only the timing matters. A price-sensitive budget can cost more if prices rise; the dashboard's purchasing-power scenario uses an assumed inflation rate. U.S. CPI-U is a national average, so it may not match a family's own costs.

    Data

    Not assessed

    • Settlement, receipt of funds, taxes, fees and minimum amounts
    • Whether a payment can be met; this page compares dates only

    The model starts at the saved quote date. Quotes are saved observations, not executable offers, and the calendar never ranks or recommends a bill.
