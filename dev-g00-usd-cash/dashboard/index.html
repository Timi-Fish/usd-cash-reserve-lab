About this dashboard

What: for a family-office client and advisor, whether saved Treasury bill maturities reach planned payment dates, and what an assumed inflation rate does to the cash meanwhile.

Data: saved U.S. Treasury daily bill rates and BLS CPI-U, dated at the top of the page. Formulas and sources are on the Method tab.

Limits: quotes are not offers; settlement, taxes and fees are not assessed; inflation is an assumption, not a forecast; no bill is ranked or recommended.

Educational project. Not investment advice.
